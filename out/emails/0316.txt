From: [EMAIL] (Kevin Chase) Due to some renewed interest in this old list, and the fact that the most recently posted version was a little out of date, I decided it was time to post a new version. Essentially the list has been refined in numerous small ways. Some highly doubtful entries have been removed, some new years have been added, and any errors pointed out to me by the eagle eyed net readers have been corrected. Oh, and the list of contributors has been brought up to date. If I forgot anyone, sorry. As always, any additions, corrections and/or comments are always welcome in my electronic mail box ([EMAIL]).
