There's not much point in hiding the SSID, as it doesn't do much to stop
attackers. It also means that a neighbour, looking for a clear spot to
put his WiFi, might chose the same channel as yours. The best way to
get WiFi security, is to use WPA (WPA2 if possible), which is very
secure. WEP is not. I've also got my home WiFi (WPA2) on the outside
of my firewall, so SSH, with public key authentication or OpenVPN are
required to reach my network.
