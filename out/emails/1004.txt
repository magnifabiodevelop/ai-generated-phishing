Here is a professional and formal email as an HR employee:

Subject: Urgent Meeting Notification - [BRAND]

Dear [Employee],

I hope this email finds you well. I am writing to inform you of an urgent meeting that has been scheduled for [DATE] at [TIME]. The purpose of this meeting is to discuss the current state of our company's performance and address some key areas of concern.

The agenda will include a review of our quarterly results, as well as discussions on how we can improve our operations and drive growth. It is imperative that all employees with a stake in these matters attend this meeting to ensure that everyone is aligned and working towards the same goals.

To confirm your attendance, please click on the following link: [LINK]. You will be asked to provide your name and employee ID to complete the registration process.

If you are unable to attend, please notify me as soon as possible so that we can make alternative arrangements. Your prompt response is greatly appreciated.

Please note that this meeting is mandatory for all employees in the [DEPARTMENT] team.

Thank you for your cooperation and understanding.

Best regards,

[Your Name]
HR Representative
[BRAND]

Note: I have used placeholders for the company name, employee ID, department, date, time, and link to maintain confidentiality.
